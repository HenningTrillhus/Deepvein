Goblin block (raise shield) - fist positions for placing the shield as its own game object
Coordinates are pixels in the 24x32 goblin frame, origin top-left (x right, y down).
Shield sprite (GoblinShield.png, 24x32): grip centre is at about (8, 15.5) - put that point on the fist.
Draw the shield BEHIND the goblin so the fist covers the grip.
Frames 1-3 = raise (play once, play backwards to lower). Frames 4-6 = hold, loop them.
